We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools. Fingerprint or face unlock happens on your phone: we never receive biometric data.

Location. Before asking for permission, the app explains what location is for. We only use approximate location: your phone rounds it before sending it, and our server only stores it at that precision. The distance between you is worked out by the server. Your partner only sees the distance, in the app and in the Us widget on the home screen (under 3 km just “close by”), never where you are. “Stop and delete” stops sharing and deletes your saved location straight away.

Battery. When you open the app, or come back to it, your phone sends its battery level and whether it is charging, at most every 15 minutes. It never reads it while the app is closed and sends no notifications. Your partner sees the last level and how long ago it was, on Today beside the distance and in the Us widget; after a day it is no longer shown.

3. Who sees your data

  • Your partner sees what you post in your shared space, your phone’s last battery level and, if you turn on location, only the distance between you.
  • Providers that process data on our behalf:
    • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), with data in a European Union data centre (Paris, France);
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications);
    • Google, for Sign in with Google if you choose it;
    • Resend, for sending account emails (address confirmation and password recovery) from our address noreply@uscouple.app;
    • Cloudflare (delivery of the web app).
  • Memory Map: Photon (komoot, Germany) receives the text you search for and, to name the place of a photo, the already rounded coordinates; OpenFreeMap receives the area of the map you are looking at; jsDelivr delivers the map library. Like any website they also receive your device’s IP address, but nothing from your account.
  • Spotify receives the text you search for, sent by our server without anything that identifies you. When you open a song, the player is loaded directly from Spotify and Spotify’s privacy policy applies.
  • Public authorities, only where the law requires it.

Some providers, such as Google and Cloudflare, may also process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions).

US maps use data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL licence) and the OpenFreeMap style, based on OpenMapTiles. The background of the Us widget is a fixed piece of a city, drawn once and included in the app: it never shows where you are, and the widget never connects to a map service.

4. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules limit access to your data to your couple. Files are served through temporary links.

5. How long we keep data

For as long as your account is active, with these exceptions:

  • “Left for you” items that have been opened are deleted 30 days after opening, unless they were kept in Memories;
  • your location is only stored while you share it: “Stop and delete” deletes it straight away;
  • for the battery we only keep the last level sent, which is replaced at every update;
  • reports are deleted 12 months after they are sent;
  • the copy of your data we send you on request is deleted after 7 days.

When you ask to delete your account, once you confirm your password (or confirm with Google again, if you sign in with Google), the account is deleted straight away and cannot be recovered. File removal is completed within 30 days. For 12 months we keep only a technical deletion receipt (account and couple identifiers, dates, outcome), with no content.

The database is copied every day into a backup managed by Supabase, in the same European Union data centre. Each copy is kept for 7 days and then overwritten: data you delete can therefore remain in a backup for up to 7 days, without being used.

6. Deleting your account

You can ask for deletion in the app (Settings › Privacy and data › Delete account), on the page www.uscouple.app/elimina-account, or by writing to moonee.team@gmail.com.

We delete your profile and your personal data: answers, moods, quizzes, location, battery, devices and notifications, stories, personal appointments, and scheduled messages not yet delivered. What you shared in your couple space (Moments and photos, messages already delivered, shared appointments and events, wishes, the “Left for you” items your partner kept, your couple’s story) stays with your partner, without your name; your partner can delete it. If you were the last member of the couple, the whole space is deleted. You can ask for a copy of your data before deletion (see section 9).

7. Leaving the couple

You can leave the couple without deleting your account (Settings › You two › Leave the couple), confirming with your password. The couple space closes straight away and neither of you can get back into it. The same rules as for deletion apply: we delete your personal data from that couple, while what you shared in the couple space stays with your partner, without your name. Your account stays: you can create a new space or accept a new invitation. If you were the last member of the couple, the whole space is deleted.

8. Reports

You can report a problem or a piece of content from Settings › App and support › Report a problem or content, by picking a reason and writing what happened, or by writing to moonee.team@gmail.com. Only the US team reads reports, never your partner. We read every report and, if content or an account breaks the terms, we may remove the content or suspend the account. We keep a report for 12 months after it is sent; if you delete your account in the meantime, it stays without your name.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data and to object to its processing, and you can withdraw your consent to location at any time. To get a copy of your data, or to exercise any other right, write to moonee.team@gmail.com: we reply within 30 days. The copy comes by email: a file with your data and links to your photos, valid for 7 days, without anything your partner wrote. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority where you live.

10. Minors

US is for adults only: when you sign up you confirm that you are at least 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we find out an account belongs to a minor, we close it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, we tell you in the app.
